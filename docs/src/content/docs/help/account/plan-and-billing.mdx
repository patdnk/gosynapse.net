**Plan & billing** is where your club manages its own Synapse subscription. You don't need to email anyone for most changes.

Select **Plan & billing** in the sidebar, under **Admin**.

Only admins for the whole organisation see it.

## What you'll find there

- **The plan card** shows your plan, its price, and the date it renews or ends. It also shows the terms you accepted.
- **Modules** are grouped by product. Each switch saves as soon as you change it.
- **Change plan** has **Upgrade** and **Downgrade**.
- **Cancel subscription** ends your subscription at the end of the period you've paid for.
- **Manage billing** opens Stripe's billing page for your card and invoices.

A module shows **Switched off by Synapse** if Synapse turned it off, and you can't switch it on. A module shows **Not part of your plan** if your plan doesn't include its product. To get one of those, upgrade, or ask Synapse.

## Switch modules on and off

Use the switch beside the module. A switched-off module disappears from everyone's navigation. Nothing is deleted, so switching it back on restores it.

## Upgrade

1. Under **Change plan**, select **Upgrade** on the higher plan.
2. Check **Due now**. Stripe works out the difference for the rest of your period.
3. Tick the box to accept the terms.
4. Select **Upgrade to** the new plan.

The new plan starts once the payment clears. If the payment fails, your plan stays as it was.

## Downgrade

1. Under **Change plan**, select **Downgrade** on the lower plan.
2. Read which modules switch off, and when.
3. Select **Downgrade on** the date shown.

A downgrade starts at the end of your current period, so you keep what you've paid for until then. Until it starts, select **Undo** to keep your current plan.

## Cancel

1. Select **Cancel subscription**.
2. Confirm in the box that opens.

Your subscription runs to the end of the period you've paid for. Then your account is suspended, and a week later it locks. See [Missed payments, suspension and lock](/docs/help/account/missed-payments-suspension-and-lock/). Select **Undo cancel** to keep going. This only works until the paid period ends.

You can't upgrade or downgrade while a cancel is pending. Undo the cancel first.

## Update your card and see invoices

Select **Manage billing**. It opens Stripe's billing page, where you can change your card, download invoices and add tax IDs. Plan changes and cancelling aren't there. Use **Plan & billing** for those.

## Request a change

If you're on Enterprise, or Synapse invoices you directly rather than charging a card, the buttons say **Request change** instead.

1. Select **Request change**.
2. Say what you'd like to change.
3. Select **Send request**.

Synapse replies by email. Plan changes are only in your hands when your plan is paid by card and the subscription is live.
