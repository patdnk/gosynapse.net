You can sign your club up yourself. You choose a plan, confirm your email address and accept the terms. Then you either pay by card and start straight away, or ask Synapse for access.

## Fill in the sign-up form

1. Go to [gosynapse.net](https://gosynapse.net) and select **Get started**.
2. Enter your **Organisation name**, **Country** and **Currency**.
3. Enter your first venue's **Venue name** and **Timezone**.
4. Enter **Your name** and your **Work email**. You become the first admin.
5. Choose a plan: **Starter**, **Pro** or **Enterprise**. Switch off any modules you don't want.
6. Optionally, add a note under **Anything we should know?**
7. Select **Sign up**.

There is no password on the form. You choose one in the next step, after you've confirmed your email.

Each plan card shows its price and any card commission. See [Card payments through Synapse](/docs/help/account/card-payments-through-synapse/). Enterprise, and any club that doesn't use pounds, shows "We'll be in touch about pricing".

## Confirm your email

We send a link to the address you entered. The page tells you to check your inbox.

1. Open the email and select the link. It works once, and for 48 hours.
2. Type a password twice. It must be at least 10 characters.
3. Select **Set password and continue**.

If the email doesn't arrive, check your spam folder. Then select **Send the link again** on the "Check your email" page. A new link replaces the old one.

If you never confirm, we remove the sign-up after 7 days. You can sign up again with the same address.

## Check your details and accept the terms

After you confirm, you land on a page that shows your club's name, plan and modules.

- If you made a mistake, select **Edit** and correct the name, currency or country.
- Read the terms and tick **I accept the terms and the pricing above.** Synapse records which version you accepted, and the price you were shown.

## Pay by card, or ask for access

You have two ways in.

### Pay and start

**Pay and start** appears when your currency is pounds and your plan can be bought by card.

1. Select **Pay and start**.
2. Enter your card on Stripe's secure page. VAT is added at checkout. Synapse never sees your card details.
3. Once Stripe confirms the payment, your club is active and the dashboard opens. This usually takes seconds.

If you leave Stripe without paying, you come back to the start page. Nothing is charged.

### Request access

Select **Request access** if you'd rather be checked first, or your plan isn't sold by card.

1. Select **Request access**.
2. Add a note for our team if you like. Synapse reviews your request, usually within one working day. We email you when we've decided.
3. You can use the dashboard while you wait. A banner says your request is under review.

While your request is under review you can build venues, menus, floor plans and your team. You can also start [card payment setup](/docs/help/account/card-payments-through-synapse/). You can't yet trade or reach outside Synapse. Pairing tills and kitchen screens, API keys, marketing and sending email wait until you're approved. Synapse sends no email on your club's behalf until then.

Synapse can approve you, approve you to pay by card, or decline the request. If it approves you to pay by card, the email links back to the start page, which shows **Pay and start** only. If it declines, the email gives the reason when there is one, and you're free to sign up again.

## Good to know

- The first time you sign in later, you may be asked to set up a second step, such as a code by text, email or authenticator app.
- Only the person who controls the email inbox can finish a sign-up. If someone else signs up with your address, you get an email telling you so, and nothing is created.
- To change your plan afterwards, see [Plan & billing](/docs/help/account/plan-and-billing/).
