The front-of-house (FOH) screen is made for the door: a tablet or screen at reception that shows who's in the building and lets you check members in fast. Staff sign in with the same email and password they use for the Synapse dashboard.

1. Open the FOH screen address you were given.
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**.
3. Select **Sign in**.

## What you see

![The FOH Who's in board](/docs/screenshots/reception/foh-board.png)

- The header shows today's date, who's on the door, how many people are in and how many VIPs. The ☀ ◐ ☾ buttons change the screen's colours to suit the room. **Sign out** is at the end.
- **Search a member, or type a code…** finds a member, or checks in a pass code. See [Check members in and out](/docs/help/reception/check-members-in/).
- **Who's in** shows a tile for everyone in the house: members, their guests (marked **Guest**, with their host) and day-pass holders. Badges flag VIPs, birthdays, first visits and members with credit. People who have left are listed under **Departed**.

Managers and admins also get a **Venue overview** tab.

## Good to know

The board updates by itself, so you can leave it open all evening.
