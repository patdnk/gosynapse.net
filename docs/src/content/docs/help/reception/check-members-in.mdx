When a member arrives, find them on the front-of-house (FOH) screen and check them in. The board then shows them in the house, and staff see their allergies and notes.

## Check a member in

1. Select the search box, **Search a member, or type a code…**, and type part of their name.
2. Select the member in the results.

   ![Searching for a member on the FOH screen](/docs/screenshots/reception/search-member.png)

3. Select **Check in**. Or select **Open details** first to see their profile.

The screen confirms it, for example "✓ Imogen Ashworth · Checked in · 22:42", with any allergy flagged.

**Other ways in:**

- Type a guest-pass code into the search box and choose **Check in with code**.
- Select **Member Check-in** and scan the member's wallet pass on the pass reader.

## Their page

Select a member's tile on the board to open their page. It shows their membership, allergies, notes for the room, bookings, tonight's guests and recent visits.

![A member's page on the FOH screen](/docs/screenshots/reception/member-page.png)

## Check a member out

On the member's page, select **Checked in — check out**. They move to **Departed** on the board.

<Aside type="caution">
Selecting a **guest's** tile on the board checks that guest out straight away. There's no confirmation.
</Aside>

## From the Synapse dashboard

**Reception** in the Synapse dashboard does the same job on a computer: search under **Check in** and select a member to check them in at once, or use **Scan / enter code**. **Today's arrivals** lists everyone in, with **Check out** next to each.
