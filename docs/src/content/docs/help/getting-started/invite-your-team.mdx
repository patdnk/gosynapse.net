Give everyone who works at your venue their own login, so the dashboard shows each person what their role needs and checks show who served them.

1. Go to **Staff**.
2. Select **Add staff member**.
3. Fill in their details:
   - **Name** and **Email**. The email is what they sign in with.
   - **Role**. See [Choose the right role](/docs/help/staff/roles-and-access/).
   - **Password**, at least 8 characters.
   - **Till PIN (optional)**, 4 to 6 digits, if they'll use the till. Someone with a PIN appears as a server on checks.
4. Select **Save**.

![The Add staff member dialog](/docs/screenshots/getting-started/add-staff.png)

## Tell them how to sign in

Synapse doesn't send an invitation email. Give the person the dashboard address, their email and their password yourself, and ask them to keep the password private.

To change someone's details later, or reset a password they've forgotten, see [Reset a password, role or till PIN](/docs/help/staff/change-password-or-pin/).
