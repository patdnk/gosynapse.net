The Synapse dashboard is where you set up your venue and look after members, bookings, checks and staff. Your admin gives you your login: the email address they added you with and a first password.

1. Open the Synapse dashboard address you were given.
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**.
3. Select **Sign in**.

![The sign-in form](/docs/screenshots/getting-started/sign-in.png)

If your organisation uses single sign-on, you'll also see **Sign in with SSO (MFA)**. Use that if your admin asked you to.

## Find your way around

After signing in you land on the **Dashboard**. Purchasers land on **Purchase orders**, where their work starts.

The sidebar groups pages the way your venue works: **Overview**, **POS**, **Catalogue**, **Procurement**, **CRM** and **Admin**.

![The dashboard after signing in, with the sidebar on the left](/docs/screenshots/getting-started/sidebar.png)

You only see the pages your role can use and your organisation has. A waiter sees a short list; an admin sees everything. See [Choose the right role](/docs/help/staff/roles-and-access/) for what each role sees.

To sign out, select **Sign out** at the bottom of the sidebar.

## Forgotten your password?

There is no reset link on the sign-in page. Ask an admin at your venue to set a new password for you: see [Reset a password, role or till PIN](/docs/help/staff/change-password-or-pin/).
