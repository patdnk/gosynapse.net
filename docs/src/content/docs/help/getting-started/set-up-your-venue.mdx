When your organisation joins Synapse, we set up your venues and the locations inside them with you: a bar, a restaurant, a terrace. Check they're right before you go further, then choose when your trading day starts.

## Check your venues and locations

1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Find **Organisation structure**. It lists each venue with its time zone, and the locations inside it.

![Organisation structure in Settings](/docs/screenshots/getting-started/organisation-structure.png)

There's no screen for adding or renaming venues and locations yourself. If something is missing or wrong, email support@gosynapse.net and we'll change it.

## Set when your trading day starts

The trading day decides which day a sale counts towards. With the default, 05:00, a sale at 01:30 counts towards the night before, so a late night's takings stay together.

1. Under the venue in **Organisation structure**, change **Trading day starts at**.
2. Select **Save**.

Each venue has its own trading day, so set it for each one.
