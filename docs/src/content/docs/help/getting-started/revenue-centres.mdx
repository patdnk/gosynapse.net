A revenue centre is a part of your business you want to see sales for separately, such as the bar, the restaurant or room service. Checks and sales reports are organised by revenue centre. Each location starts with one revenue centre of its own, so you only need more if you want to split a location further.

## Add a revenue centre

1. Go to **Revenue centers**.

   ![The Revenue centers list](/docs/screenshots/getting-started/revenue-centres.png)

2. Select **Add revenue center**.
3. Choose the **Venue**.
4. Enter a **Name**, such as "Terrace Bar", and optionally a short **Code**, such as "TBAR".
5. Choose its **Physical home (location)**, or leave it venue-wide.
6. Keep **Active** ticked.
7. Select **Save**.

![The Add revenue center dialog](/docs/screenshots/getting-started/add-revenue-centre.png)

## Link it to your floors

A check takes its revenue centre from the table it's opened on. Each table belongs to an area, and each area to a floor. Give floors, or single areas, a revenue centre:

1. Go to **Floor plans** and choose the location.
2. Select the floor, then **Edit floor**.
3. Choose the **Revenue center** and select **Save**.

An area can override its floor's revenue centre: select **edit** on the area and choose **Revenue center override (optional)**.

The **Floors** and **Areas** columns on **Revenue centers** show how many use each one.
