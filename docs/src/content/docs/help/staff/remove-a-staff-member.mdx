When someone leaves, or is away for a long time, stop them signing in. You can deactivate them, which keeps them on the list, or remove them.

## Deactivate

1. Go to **Staff** and select **Edit** next to the person.
2. Untick **Active**.
3. Select **Save**.

Their status changes to **Inactive** and they're signed out. To let them back in later, tick **Active** again.

## Remove

1. Go to **Staff** and select **Remove** next to the person.
2. Confirm. Synapse warns that they won't be able to sign in.

They disappear from the list and can no longer sign in.

![The Staff list](/docs/screenshots/staff/list.png)

## Good to know

- You can't deactivate or remove yourself. Ask another admin.
- Removing someone doesn't change past checks: their name stays on the checks they served.
