Every member of staff has one role. The role decides which pages they see in the Synapse dashboard and what they can change there. Choose the narrowest role that lets someone do their job.

## What each role sees

| Role | Sees in the dashboard |
|---|---|
| **Waiter** | Dashboard, Checks, Reception, Bookings, Guests, Events, Cloakroom |
| **Manager** | Everything a waiter sees, plus Reports, Insights, Floor plans, Revenue centers, Discounts, Kitchen screens, Catalogue, Procurement, Applicants, Members, Membership plans, Loyalty, Wallet passes and Marketing |
| **Admin** | Everything, including Accounting, Till layouts, Staff, Integrations and Settings |
| **Financial** | Dashboard, Reports, Insights, Accounting, Checks and Procurement |
| **Purchaser** | Catalogue and Procurement only, and starts on Purchase orders |

Pages also depend on what your organisation has. A page for a product or feature you don't have is hidden for everyone, whatever their role. For example, an organisation without POS has no Checks or Floor plans.

## Good to know

- Only admins can add staff, change roles and change settings.
- Seeing a page doesn't always mean being able to change everything on it. Where that matters, the article for that task says who can do it, under **Who can do this**.
- Waiters, managers and admins can also be given a till PIN to unlock the till. See [Reset a password, role or till PIN](/docs/help/staff/change-password-or-pin/).
