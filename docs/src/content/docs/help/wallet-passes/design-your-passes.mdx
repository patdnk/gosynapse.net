Members can keep a pass for your club in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet and show it at the door. You design each kind of pass once; every pass issued after that uses the design.

1. Go to **Wallet passes**. The **Design** tab opens.
2. Choose the kind of pass: **Membership**, **Loyalty card**, **Day pass** or **Guest pass**.
3. Set how it looks:
   - **Display name (club name on the pass)** and an optional **Tagline**.
   - A **Logo URL**, and images for the Apple strip and Google hero.
   - **Background**, **Text** and **Labels** colours.
   - The **Barcode format** the door scans.
4. Under **Fields shown on the pass**, choose what members see, such as their membership tier, member number or loyalty credit.
5. Check the Apple and Google previews, then select **Save Membership design** (the button names the kind of pass you're editing).

![Designing a membership pass](/docs/screenshots/wallet-passes/design.png)

## Good to know

- Managers can view designs but only admins can change them.
- Passes have to be signed with your club's Apple and Google credentials before members can add them to their phones. Until that's set up, the page says "Stub mode". The **Certificates** tab shows what's configured; email support@gosynapse.net to set it up.
