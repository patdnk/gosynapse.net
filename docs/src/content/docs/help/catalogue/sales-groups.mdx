Sales groups decide how sales are totalled: food against drinks, starters against mains. Each menu item belongs to one group, and each group posts to its own income account in your accounts. Items with no group land in the default group, **Other**.

## Add a sales group

1. Go to **Catalogue**, then the **Sales groups** tab.
2. Select **Add sales group**.
3. Enter a **Name**, such as "Bar snacks", an optional short **Code (optional)** and a **Sort order**.
4. Select **Save**.

![The Sales groups list](/docs/screenshots/catalogue/sales-groups.png)

## Put items in a group

Set the **Sales group** on each menu item: go to **Menu items**, select **Edit** on the item and choose the group. See [Add a dish or drink to the menu](/docs/help/catalogue/add-a-menu-item/).

## Retire a group

Select **Deactivate** on a group you no longer use, and **Activate** to bring it back. The default group can't be deactivated.
