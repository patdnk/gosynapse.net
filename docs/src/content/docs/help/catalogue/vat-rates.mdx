Each venue has its own list of VAT rates. Every price on a menu uses one of them, so receipts and the accounts show the right VAT.

1. Go to **Catalogue**, then the **Tax rates** tab.
2. Choose the **Venue**.
3. Select **Add tax rate**.
4. Enter a **Name**, such as "Reduced rate".
5. Choose the **VAT treatment**: **Standard rate**, **Reduced rate**, **Zero-rated**, **Exempt** or **Outside the scope of VAT**.
6. Enter the **Rate %**. It's switched off for zero-rated, exempt and out-of-scope rates, which are always 0%.
7. Tick **Default rate** if this is the rate most items use.
8. Select **Save**.

![The Tax rates list](/docs/screenshots/catalogue/tax-rates.png)

## Good to know

- Rates can be changed with **Edit** but not deleted.
- Choose the rate for each item when you put it on a menu. See [Add a dish or drink to the menu](/docs/help/catalogue/add-a-menu-item/).
