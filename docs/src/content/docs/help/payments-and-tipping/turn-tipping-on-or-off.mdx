When tipping is on, the till offers guests a tip when they pay by card. It's on unless you turn it off.

1. Go to **Settings** and select **Open configurator**.
2. Under **Payments**, switch **Offer tipping on card payments** on or off.

![The tipping switch in the configurator](/docs/screenshots/payments-and-tipping/tipping-toggle.png)

The change takes effect straight away. There is no save button.

## What staff see on the till

With tipping on, a card or card-reader payment shows an **Add a tip** step with **None**, **5%**, **10%**, **12.5%** or a custom amount. The tip is added on top of the amount being paid, and the charge button shows the total including the tip. Cash payments have no tip step.

The percentages are fixed. Tips appear in the **Tips** column of **Reports**, if Reports is part of your plan.
