Card readers take card-present payments: the guest taps or inserts a card on the reader. Synapse works with Stripe and Adyen readers. Each location has a default reader, which the till uses when staff choose a card-reader payment.

## Before you start

Your Stripe or Adyen account must be connected. See [Connect a payment processor](/docs/help/payments-and-tipping/connect-a-payment-processor/). The reader needs to be switched on and showing its registration code.

## Register the reader

1. Go to **Settings** and find **Card readers (terminals)**.
2. Select **Register reader**.
3. Choose the **Location** the reader is in and its **Provider**.
4. Give it a **Label** staff will recognise, such as "Front counter".
5. Enter the **Registration code** the reader shows.
6. Tick **Default reader for this location** if the till should use this reader.
7. Select **Register**.

![The Register reader dialog](/docs/screenshots/payments-and-tipping/register-reader.png)

The reader appears in the list with its location and status. To retire a reader, select **Delete** next to it.
