The organisation configurator shows what your Synapse plan includes. It's also where you switch connectors and a few organisation-wide options on or off.

1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Under **Organisation**, select **Open configurator**.

![The Organisation configurator](/docs/screenshots/settings/configurator.png)

## What you'll find there

- **Subscription** names your plan and the products it includes.
- **Products** lists each product as **Included** or not, with the features inside it. A product or feature you don't have is hidden from everyone's navigation.
- **Connectors** switch third-party integrations (SevenRooms, Tripleseat, Salesforce, Stripe, Adyen) on or off.
- **Receipts** cover printed receipts and kitchen chits. Most venues use digital receipts and need no printer. Turn on **Physical receipts (on-premise server)** only if you run an on-site server with receipt printers.
- **Payments** has the tipping switch. See [Turn tipping on or off](/docs/help/payments-and-tipping/turn-tipping-on-or-off/).
- **Add-ons** shows paid extras such as offline mode, which keeps the till taking orders through an internet outage.

A switch takes effect as soon as you change it. There is no separate save button.

## Good to know

Your plan and products are set by Synapse, not from inside your organisation. To add a product or change your plan, email support@gosynapse.net.
