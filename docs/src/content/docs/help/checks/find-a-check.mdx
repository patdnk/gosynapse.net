Checks are opened, added to and paid on the till. In the Synapse dashboard you can find any check to answer a question about it: what was ordered, who served it, where it moved and how much it came to.

1. Go to **Checks**. The list starts on open checks.
2. To see others, change the status filter to **All statuses**, **SETTLED** or **VOIDED**.
3. To narrow it down, choose a location instead of **All locations**.
4. Select a check to open it.

![The Checks list](/docs/screenshots/checks/list.png)

## What a check shows

- The header shows where the check is, its table, who opened it and when, the server, and any member attached.
- The lines show name, quantity, price, VAT rate and status. A voided line is struck through with its void reason.
- **Discounts** lists any discounts applied.
- **Movement trail** lists every move, transfer and merge, with who did it and when.
- The totals show the subtotal, discounts, total and the VAT included.

![A check's detail page](/docs/screenshots/checks/detail.png)

**Digital receipt** shows the receipt as the guest gets it, and **Printout** shows the printed version.

## Good to know

- The list shows the latest 100 checks for the filters you've chosen.
- Open checks update on their own as the till changes them.
- Managers and admins can also move, merge, void and reopen checks. See [Move, transfer or merge a check](/docs/help/checks/move-or-merge-a-check/) and [Void or reopen a check](/docs/help/checks/void-or-reopen-a-check/).
