1. Go to **Bookings** and select **New booking**.
2. Set the **Date**, **Time**, **Party size** and **Duration**.
3. Choose the **Location**.
4. Select **Find tables**. Synapse lists the tables there, marking each one **available** or booked.
5. Choose an available table. **Create booking** stays greyed out until you do.
6. Say who it's for:
   - For a member, search under **Member (optional)** and select them.
   - For anyone else, fill in **Guest contact (optional)**: first and last name, email and mobile. These are used for the confirmation and reminder.
7. Tick the marketing and WhatsApp boxes only if the guest said yes.
8. Add any **Notes**, such as an occasion or a high chair.
9. Select **Create booking**.

![The New booking dialog with tables found](/docs/screenshots/bookings/new-booking.png)

## Good to know

- Synapse won't book a party bigger than the table, or a table that's already booked at that time.
- A booking can't be changed after it's made. To move it, cancel it and book again. See [Run the day's bookings](/docs/help/bookings/run-the-days-bookings/).
