## Add a member

1. Go to **Members** and select **Add member**.
2. Enter their **First name**, **Last name**, **Email** and **Telephone**. Select **Add photo** to add a picture, which helps the door recognise them.
3. Choose their **Type** and **Status**. A new member is usually **Live**.
4. Fill in whatever else you know: **Join date**, **Date of birth**, **Gender**, **Occupation**, **Industry** and **Address**.
5. Record **Allergies** (for example "nuts, shellfish"), **Likes**, **Dislikes** and **Notes**. Allergies show in red on their profile and on the front-of-house screen.
6. Select **Create member**.

![The Add member dialog](/docs/screenshots/members/add-member.png)

To put them on a paid membership, see [Put a member on a plan](/docs/help/members/put-a-member-on-a-plan/).

## Change a member's details

Open the member's profile, select **Edit**, change what you need and select **Save**.

## Good to know

If your club uses Salesforce, **Sync from Salesforce** on the Members page brings in members from there.
