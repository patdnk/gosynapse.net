A membership plan charges the member's dues to their card on file, monthly or yearly. Everything happens in the **Membership** panel of the member's profile.

## Before you start

- Your club needs at least one active membership plan, under **Membership plans**.
- The member needs a card on file, shown under **Card on file**. **Subscribe** stays greyed out until there is one. Taking a member's card in the dashboard isn't available yet; to set one up, email support@gosynapse.net.

## Subscribe

1. Open the member's profile and find **Membership**.
2. Select **Subscribe**.
3. Choose the **Plan**.
4. Select **Subscribe**.

The first period is charged to their card straight away. The panel then shows the plan, the next bill date and each dues invoice.

![The Membership panel of a subscribed member](/docs/screenshots/members/membership.png)

## Change, freeze or cancel

- **Change plan**: choose the **New plan**, and whether it starts now, crediting the unused days, or at renewal. Then select **Change plan now**.
- **Freeze**: pause the membership **From** a date **Until** a date (or until you resume it), choose what happens to the unused days, add a **Reason** and select **Freeze now**.
- **Cancel**: by default the membership stays active until the end of the period you've been paid for. Tick **Cancel immediately** to end it now. Select **Cancel membership**, or **Keep membership** if you changed your mind.

Refunding to the card is only offered to admins and finance staff.
