1. Go to **Members**.
2. Type part of a name, email or membership reference into **Search by name, email or reference…**.
3. Select **View** next to the member.

![The Members list](/docs/screenshots/members/list.png)

The list shows each member's **Type** (VIP, Regular or Friend), **Status** (Live, Expired, Frozen, Missed payment or Suspended), email and join date.

## What the profile shows

A member's profile is one page:

- Their details, with any **Allergies** in red at the top so nobody misses them.
- **Membership**: their plan, card on file and dues invoices. See [Put a member on a plan](/docs/help/members/put-a-member-on-a-plan/).
- **House account**: what they owe the club, or the club owes them.
- **Wallet passes**: the passes issued to them. See [Give a member a wallet pass](/docs/help/wallet-passes/issue-a-pass/).
- **Member credit**: stored credit they can spend at the venue.
- **Feedback & cases**: feedback and complaints logged against them.
